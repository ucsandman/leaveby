The trick

  1. Every other timer counts up from the moment you started.
  2. LeaveBy plans backwards from the moment you have to leave, so it always knows whether the rest of the plan still fits.
  3. When the plan stops fitting, it drops a step you marked optional and says which one it dropped.

How it works

1

Set the leave time and the steps.

Shoes, coat, teeth, whatever your mornings are made of. Two minutes, once. Mark the steps you can live without.

2

Hand the kid the screen.

One giant card at a time. They tap it when the step is done. Finish early and the spare minutes get banked where they can watch the pile grow.

What this is not

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No download. It is a web page.
  • No subscription. It is free and it stays free.
  • It lives on the one device on your counter. Nothing leaves it.
  • The share link is a copy, not a sync. Edit yours and theirs stays as it was.
